Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatLeaf ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the ChatLeaf mobile application (the "App"), including its chat, group chat, and Status features.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a ChatLeaf account and use the App, we collect:

  • Your email address, used for sign-up, login, OTP verification, and account notifications
  • A securely hashed password — we never store or transmit your password in plain text
  • A unique User ID that you choose and can customize
  • Profile information you choose to add, such as a profile picture and an "about" line
  • The messages you send and receive, including text and any photos or media shared in one-on-one chats, group chats, and Status updates, so they can be delivered to the right recipients
  • Group membership information — which groups you're in and your role (member or admin)
  • Basic device information (device name/ID) used to detect logins from new or unrecognized devices

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure your account
  • Authenticate you when you sign up or log in, including sending OTPs
  • Deliver your one-on-one chats and group messages to their intended recipients
  • Show your Status updates to your contacts
  • Send important account emails — verification codes, new-device login alerts, and password or User ID change confirmations
  • Investigate and act on reports of abuse, harassment, or violations of our Terms & Conditions
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App

3. How We Protect Your Information

Your password is hashed using industry-standard encryption and is never stored or transmitted in plain text. Session tokens are used to keep you logged in securely, and we monitor for logins from unrecognized devices so we can alert you of potential unauthorized access. Access to user data within our systems is restricted to what is needed to operate and support the Service.

4. Chats, Groups, and Status

Messages you send in one-on-one chats and group chats are stored so they can be delivered to and displayed for the intended recipients or group members. Status updates are visible to the contacts you're connected with on ChatLeaf. We do not use the content of your private messages for advertising.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share information:

  • With the other participants of a chat or group, to the extent needed to deliver your messages and Status updates
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid request from a public authority
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of ChatLeaf, our users, or the public

6. Email Communications

We send transactional emails related to your account — such as verification codes, new-device login alerts, and password or User ID change confirmations. These emails are necessary for account security and are not promotional.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information, chats, group memberships, and Status updates for as long as your account is active. Temporary data, such as one-time verification codes, is automatically deleted shortly after use or expiry. If you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain certain records by law.

8. Children's Privacy

ChatLeaf is not intended for children under the age we specify in our Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If we become aware that we have, we will take steps to delete it.

9. Your Rights

You can update your profile information (User ID, password, about, profile picture) at any time from within the App. You can leave any group at any time. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, please contact us via our Contact page.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or our practices — for example, if we add new features in the future. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information on how we handle your information.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via our Contact page.

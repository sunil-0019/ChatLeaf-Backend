Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatLeaf ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a ChatLeaf account, we collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • A securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text)
  • A unique User ID that you can customize
  • Profile information you choose to add (profile picture, about text)
  • Basic device information (device name/ID) to detect logins from new devices

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you when you log in
  • Send you important account emails (verification codes, security alerts, password change confirmations)
  • Improve and maintain the App

3. How We Protect Your Information

Your password is hashed using industry-standard encryption (bcrypt) and is never stored or transmitted in plain text. Session tokens are used to keep you logged in securely, and we monitor for logins from unrecognized devices to alert you of potential unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share information when required by law or to protect the safety and security of our users.

5. Email Communications

We send transactional emails related to your account — such as verification codes, new-device login alerts, and password/User ID change confirmations. These emails are necessary for account security and are not promotional.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Temporary data (such as one-time verification codes) is automatically deleted shortly after use or expiry.

7. Your Rights

You can update your profile information (User ID, password, about, profile picture) at any time from within the App. If you'd like your account deleted, please contact us via the Contact page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information on our privacy practices.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.

Privacy Policy

A plain-language, complete account of what ChatLeaf collects, why, and how it's protected.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatLeaf ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you create an account and use the ChatLeaf mobile application (the "App") or this website. We built ChatLeaf around a simple idea — you should be able to message the people who matter to you without wondering what happens to your information behind the scenes. This policy is our attempt to answer that question fully, not in vague terms.

By creating a ChatLeaf account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not create an account or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Account details: your full name, email address, and a password (which we never see or store in readable form — see Section 4).
  • ChatLeaf ID: a unique, human-readable identifier you choose, used instead of a phone number so people can find and message you.
  • Profile information: anything you choose to add, such as a profile picture or an "About" line. These are entirely optional.
  • Messages you send: the content of chats, group messages and Status posts you create, along with any photos or media attached to them.
  • Support requests: anything you tell us through the Contact page or by emailing us — including your email, phone number and the message itself.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: a device identifier and device name, used specifically to recognise "your" devices and flag logins from new ones.
  • Usage and log data: basic technical logs (timestamps, error reports, API request metadata) that help us keep the service running and catch problems quickly.
  • Approximate login context: information tied to sign-in events (such as IP-derived region and device type) used only for the new-device security alert described below.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your phone number to create or use an account, we do not access your device's contact list without a separate, explicit permission prompt, and we do not track your location in the background.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above for clearly defined purposes, and nothing beyond them:

  • Creating, authenticating and maintaining your account.
  • Delivering your messages, Status posts and group content to the people you send them to.
  • Sending essential account emails: sign-up verification codes, password-reset codes, new-device login alerts, and confirmation emails when your password or ChatLeaf ID changes.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, spam and unauthorised access attempts.
  • Responding to support requests submitted through the Contact page.
  • Diagnosing bugs, measuring reliability, and generally keeping the App fast and stable.
  • Meeting legal obligations where we are required to by law.

We do not use your messages, photos or profile information to build advertising profiles, and ChatLeaf does not run third-party ad networks inside the App.

4. How We Protect Your Information

Security isn't a single feature in ChatLeaf — it's built into how the account system works:

  • Password hashing: your password is hashed with a strong, industry-standard algorithm before it's ever stored. We cannot see your actual password, and neither can anyone with access to our database.
  • Session tokens: logging in issues a secure access token and a refresh token; refresh tokens are stored in hashed form, the same way passwords are, so a database copy alone cannot be used to impersonate you.
  • New-device alerts: the first time your account is used from a device we don't recognise, we email you immediately so you can act if it wasn't you.
  • Rate limiting: sign-up, login, and one-time-code requests are all rate-limited to make automated guessing and abuse impractical.
  • Encrypted media storage: profile pictures and shared media are hosted through a dedicated, access-controlled media provider rather than sitting in an open folder.

No system is ever perfectly unbreakable, and we won't claim ours is. What we can commit to is that security is treated as a first-class requirement, not an afterthought, and that we will tell you promptly if something affecting your account ever needs your attention.

5. One-Time Codes & Temporary Data

Verification codes used for sign-up and password recovery are short-lived by design: each code expires automatically a few minutes after it's issued, and is deleted from our systems once it's used or once it expires — whichever comes first. We do not keep a history of past codes. To prevent abuse, we do enforce sensible limits on how often codes can be requested (a short minimum gap between requests, and a temporary lock after repeated attempts within a day), but the codes themselves are never retained beyond their short lifetime.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we never will. Limited sharing does happen in a small number of specific cases:

  • Service providers: we use a small number of infrastructure providers to run ChatLeaf — a database host, an email delivery service for account emails, and a media hosting provider for profile pictures and shared images. Each only receives the minimum information needed to perform its function, and none are permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Other users: your ChatLeaf ID, profile picture, About text and Status posts are visible to the contacts and groups you interact with, because that's the entire point of a chat app. Your email address and password are never shared with other users.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety or property of ChatLeaf, our users, or the public.
  • Business changes: if ChatLeaf were ever involved in a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, user information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to the same protections described here.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your profile, credentials and associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where a short retention window is necessary for security logs, fraud prevention, or a legal obligation. Messages you've sent may still be visible to the recipient's copy of the conversation, in the same way that a message you've sent in any chat app remains with the person you sent it to.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You're in control of your ChatLeaf profile at all times:

  • Update your profile picture, About text and password at any time from within the App.
  • Change your ChatLeaf ID once every month, directly from your settings.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data, by reaching out through our Contact page.

We will respond to any privacy request as promptly as we reasonably can, and will let you know if we need more information to verify your identity before acting on it.

9. Children's Privacy

ChatLeaf is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. If we become aware that we've collected information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly.

10. International Users

ChatLeaf's infrastructure may process and store information in countries other than the one you live in. Wherever your information is processed, we apply the same standards of protection described in this policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as ChatLeaf grows or as regulations change. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where appropriate, notify you directly. Continuing to use ChatLeaf after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out through our Contact page — we read every message personally.

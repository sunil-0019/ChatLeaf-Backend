Why people choose ChatLeaf

ChatLeaf is built around messaging done well, rather than a long list of features nobody uses. Sign-up and login are protected with one-time passcodes (OTP), passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text, and we alert you whenever your account is opened from a new device. There's no voice or video calling in ChatLeaf by design — the app stays focused on chat, groups, and Status, so it's lighter, simpler, and easier to trust.

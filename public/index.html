Everything a chat app needs, nothing it doesn't

ChatLeaf keeps things simple on purpose — messaging, status and groups, with no clutter and no noisy extras.

Secure sign-up

Every account is verified by email before it's ever created, so there's no room for throwaway or fake accounts.

🟢

Google Sign-In

Log in with your email and password, or continue with Google in one tap — same secure account either way.

🏷️

Your own ChatLeaf ID

A short, memorable ID that people use to find and add you — no phone number required, ever.

📶

New-device alerts

The moment your account is opened on an unfamiliar device, you'll get an email so you can act fast.

Status updates

Share a quick photo, thought or update that your contacts can see — gone again after it's had its moment.

👥

Groups

Bring people together in one thread for trips, teams or family — built for text, not for calls.

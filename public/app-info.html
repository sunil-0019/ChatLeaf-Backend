🌿 About the app

ChatLeaf, explained from the inside out

What ChatLeaf does, how it's built, and what to expect the first time you open it — chats, Status and groups, without any calling clutter.

What is ChatLeaf?

ChatLeaf is an Android messaging app built around three things: one-to-one chats, Status updates, and groups. There's no voice or video calling anywhere in the app — the entire experience is designed around text, photos and presence, kept fast and free of noise. Every account starts with a verified email and a password, and from there you're reachable only through a short, memorable ChatLeaf ID rather than a phone number.

💬

Chats

Clean, fast one-to-one conversations with delivery and read ticks, so you always know where a message stands.

🌱

Status

Post a photo or a short update for your contacts to see — a lightweight way to share what you're up to.

👥

Groups

Create a group for your circle — family, friends, teammates — and keep every reply in one thread.

🆔

ChatLeaf ID

A unique, editable ID that stands in for a phone number. Share it however you like, keep your number private.

🔑

Google Sign-In

Skip the password entirely and continue with your Google account — it links straight to the same secure profile.

🛎️

Security alerts

New-device logins, password changes and ID changes all trigger an email, so nothing happens on your account without you knowing.

How ChatLeaf works

A quick look at the flow from install to your first conversation.

01

Sign up & verify

Enter your name, email and password, confirm a one-time code sent to your inbox, and your account is live.

02

Set up your profile

Add a profile photo and an About line, and choose your ChatLeaf ID — you can update it later, once a month.

03

Chat, post, group up

Message contacts directly, share a Status, or start a group — everything lives in one simple chat list.
